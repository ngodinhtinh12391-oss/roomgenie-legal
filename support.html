Frequently asked questions

How do I redesign a room?

Tap Redesign a room, take or choose a photo, pick a style and tap Generate. No camera? Use Try a demo room on the Home screen.

How many free designs do I get?

Your first 3 previews are free. A failed or cancelled design does not use a preview.

What does RoomGenie Pro include?

Full-resolution downloads to Photos and up to 20 HD renders a day, as a weekly or yearly subscription.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Restore: open the paywall or Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Cancel: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are sent for redesign only after you agree in the app, and uploaded photos are deleted from our servers within 48 hours. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings › Delete all data. This removes your photos, designs and anonymous account from our servers.
