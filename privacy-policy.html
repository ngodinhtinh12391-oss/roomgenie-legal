Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

RoomGenie AI ("the app", "we") is developed by Ngo Tinh. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Contact: kendyngo123@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Room photos you choose to redesign. A photo is only uploaded after you agree on the in-app consent screen and start a redesign.
  • Generated designs created from your photos.
  • An anonymous account ID. The app signs you in anonymously (Firebase Authentication) so your photos, designs and free previews belong to you. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Subscription information from Apple (transaction and expiry details) so we can unlock RoomGenie Pro. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • The date you gave consent to AI photo processing.

We do not collect your contacts, location, advertising identifier or browsing history. We do not show ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: generating your room designs, keeping your designs and boards, counting free previews and daily limits, unlocking your subscription, preventing abuse, and answering support requests.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud (United States): sign-in, storage of uploaded photos and generated designs, and our servers. App Check uses Apple's App Attest to confirm requests come from the genuine app.
  • fal.ai (United States): our AI image provider. We send the uploaded room photo and the chosen style to fal.ai only to generate your design. See fal.ai's privacy policy.
  • Apple: in-app purchases and subscriptions, under Apple's privacy policy.

Your data may be processed in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded room photos are deleted from our servers automatically within 48 hours.
  • Generated designs are kept so you can open them again, until you delete your data. Copies are also stored on your device.
  • Account and subscription records are kept until you delete your data.

Your choices

  • Delete all data: in the app, open Settings › Delete all data. This permanently removes your uploaded photos, generated designs, profile and anonymous account from our servers and clears them from the app.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription.
  • You can contact us at any time to ask what data we hold about you or to request deletion. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Shopping links

"Similar items" open retailer websites (such as IKEA, Wayfair or Amazon) in your browser. We may earn a commission from purchases. Those sites have their own privacy policies; we do not share your photos or account with them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos and designs are stored in private storage that only your account can use through the app.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
